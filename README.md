    Red-Eyes Awakening (Duelist Emperor)
        Quick-Play Spell
        Send 1 "Red-Eyes" monster from your hand or Deck to the GY; add 1 "Red-Eyes" card from your Deck or GY with a different name than the sent card to your hand. 
        You cannot Special Summon from the Extra Deck during the turn you activate this effect, except Fusion Monsters. 
        You can banish this card from your GY, then target 1 "Red-Eyes" monster you control; inflict damage to your opponent equal to half that monster's ATK. 
        You can only use each effect of "Red-Eyes Awakening" once per turn.
    Red-Eyes Ritual (Duelist Emperor)
        Ritual Spell
        Send 1 "Red-Eyes" monster from your Deck to the GY to Ritual Summon a "Lord of The Red" from your Deck. 
        You can banish this card from your GY; Special Summon a monster that mentions "The Claw of Hermos" from your Extra Deck, ignoring its Summoning conditions.
    The Roar of Hermos (Duelist Emperor)
        Quick-Play Spell
        (This card is always treated as a "Red-Eyes" card.)	
        Target 1 "Red-Eyes" monster you control or in your GY; Fusion Summon 1 Fusion Monster from your Extra Deck that mentions that monster as material, by shuffling it into the Deck as material, but banish the Summoned monster during the End Phase of the next turn. 
        You can banish this card from your GY; Special Summon 1 "Legendary Knight Hermos" from your Deck, hand, or GY, ignoring its Summoning conditions.

    Red-Eyes Ultimate Dragon, the Infernal Rival (XAX4Games)
        FIRE Dragon
        Fusion Effect
        Level 12
        "Red-Eyes Ultimate Dragon" + 3 "Red-Eyes" monsters	
        Must be either Fusion Summoned, or Special Summoned (from your Extra Deck or GY) by banishing the above cards from your field and/or GY. 
        When this card is Special Summoned: You can send all other cards you control to the GY; banish all cards your opponent controls, then inflict 300 damage to them for each card banished. 
        Each time your opponent takes effect damage: Increase this card's ATK equal to the damage they took. 
        During the End Phase of the next turn after this card was Special Summoned, send it to the GY, then Special Summon up to 3 "Red-Eyes" monsters from your GY.
    Red-Eyes Alternative Ultimate Dragon (Duelist Emperor)
        DARK Dragon
        Fusion Effect
        Level 12

        If this Fusion Summoned card leaves the field: You can Special Summon 1 "Red-Eyes" monster from your hand, Deck, GY, or Extra Deck, ignoring its Summoning conditions.
